    Getting a reading you can trust

    1. Work down the sheet from the top left, one or two rows per photo. You don't need to check each photo: every row goes into the list below, and its photo is kept on this phone for 7 days so you can check it later.
    2. Fill the frame with the row. Any way up is fine; the page turns it.
    3. Keep each row whole, with its head name, inside the photo. Rows cut by the edge are skipped.
    4. Hold the phone flat over the sheet, not at an angle. If your shadow falls on the row, stand back and zoom in instead of moving closer.
    5. No flash and no glare patch on the row. Tilt the sheet or move until the shine is gone.
    6. Write your marks beside the row, not on the patterns.
